About

Hi, I’m Dženana

I’m a Senior SDET from Bosnia and Herzegovina, and I’ve spent the last six years making sure software does what it promises, and helping the people who build it get better at it too.

I’ve worked across the whole testing stack: web automation with Playwright, Cypress and Selenium, API testing, mobile and performance testing. Lately I’ve been exploring how AI can help engineering teams move faster without letting quality slip.

But the part of my work I love most is people. I’ve mentored juniors on my teams, taught testing courses, and founded the BrowserStack meetup community in Bosnia and Herzegovina, so testers here have a place to learn, share and grow together.

Why this blog#

After years of answering the same good questions from mentees, I wanted one place to share what I’ve learned: honest opinions on testing, practical lessons from real projects, and the things I wish someone had told me earlier.

I also write about being a woman in IT, and in QA specifically. Too many talented women are told testing is the “easier” path, or underestimated once they’re in it. I want this to be a place that says otherwise.

What you’ll find here#

  • Opinions on test automation and QA practice
  • Lessons from mentoring and teaching
  • Stories from the BiH testing community
  • Career growth for testers, especially women in tech
